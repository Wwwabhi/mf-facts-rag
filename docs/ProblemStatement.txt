FACTS-ONLY MUTUAL FUND ASSISTANT

1. Problem

Retail investors often need quick answers to simple factual questions about mutual fund schemes, such as expense ratio, exit load, minimum SIP amount, benchmark, riskometer, ELSS lock-in period, and how to download account or capital-gains statements.

Finding these facts can require going through multiple scheme pages, factsheets, KIM/SID documents, AMC websites, SEBI resources, and AMFI resources. The information may also be spread across different documents, making it difficult to find a concise and reliable answer quickly.

The goal is to build a small RAG-based FAQ assistant that helps users retrieve verified facts about selected mutual fund schemes from official public sources.

2. Product Context

Product: Groww

The assistant is being designed in the context of a Groww-like mutual fund experience, but Groww pages will not be used as factual knowledge sources for the RAG system.

The assistant will use only verified public information from:
- HDFC Mutual Fund / HDFC AMC
- SEBI
- AMFI

3. Corpus Scope

AMC:
HDFC Mutual Fund

Selected schemes:
1. HDFC Large Cap Fund
2. HDFC Flexi Cap Fund
3. HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund
4. HDFC Mid Cap Fund

The knowledge corpus will contain 15–25 official public sources, including relevant:
- Scheme pages
- Factsheets
- Key Information Memorandums (KIM)
- Scheme Information Documents (SID)
- Scheme FAQs
- Fee and charges information
- Riskometer and benchmark information
- Investor service pages
- Account statement and capital-gains statement guides
- Relevant SEBI and AMFI investor education or regulatory pages

4. Target Users

Primary users:
- Retail users who want quick factual information about mutual fund schemes.
- Users comparing factual scheme details without asking for investment recommendations.

Secondary users:
- Support teams
- Content teams
- Teams answering repetitive mutual fund information queries

5. Product Goal

Build a simple FAQ assistant that can retrieve relevant information from the selected official source corpus and provide concise, citation-backed answers to factual mutual fund questions.

The assistant should make it easier for users to find verified information without requiring them to search through multiple long documents.

6. Example Questions the Assistant Should Answer

The assistant should be able to answer factual questions such as:

- What is the expense ratio of HDFC Large Cap Fund?
- What is the exit load of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?
- What is the minimum SIP amount for HDFC Mid Cap Fund?
- What is the lock-in period for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
- What is the riskometer level of the scheme?
- What is the benchmark of the scheme?
- What is the minimum investment amount?
- What is the investment objective of the scheme?
- How can I download a capital-gains statement?
- How can I download an account statement?

7. Answer Requirements

For factual questions, the assistant must:

- Answer using only information retrieved from the approved official sources.
- Keep the answer concise and limited to 3 sentences or fewer.
- Include exactly one clear source link.
- Include the text:
  "Last updated from sources: <date>"
- Avoid unsupported claims or information not found in the available sources.
- Say that the information could not be verified from the available sources when the retrieved information does not support an answer.

8. Investment Advice Boundary

The assistant is facts-only and must not provide investment advice.

It must refuse questions such as:

- Should I buy this mutual fund?
- Should I sell this mutual fund?
- Which fund should I invest in?
- Which fund is better?
- Is this a good mutual fund?
- Is this fund suitable for me?
- What should I invest in based on my income or age?
- Which scheme will give me the highest returns?

The refusal should be polite and explain that the assistant provides factual information only.

Where appropriate, the refusal should provide a relevant official investor-education source from AMFI or SEBI.

9. Performance and Returns Boundary

The assistant must not:

- Calculate investment returns.
- Compare scheme performance.
- Predict future returns.
- Recommend a scheme based on historical performance.
- State how much money a user will earn.

Questions about returns or performance should receive a facts-only refusal or be redirected to the relevant official factsheet or source where appropriate.

10. Privacy and PII

The assistant must not accept, request, process, or store personally identifiable or account-specific information.

This includes:
- PAN
- Aadhaar
- OTP
- Bank details
- Mutual fund account numbers
- Phone numbers
- Email addresses

If a user provides such information, the assistant should not process it and should respond with a safe message explaining that only general factual scheme information can be provided.

11. Source Policy

Only approved official sources may be used by the RAG system.

Allowed publishers:
- HDFC Mutual Fund / HDFC AMC
- SEBI
- AMFI

Third-party blogs, financial websites, forums, social media posts, and other unofficial sources must not be used as knowledge sources.

Every factual answer must be traceable to one of the approved sources.

12. RAG Requirement

The assistant should use a Retrieval-Augmented Generation architecture.

The ingestion pipeline should follow:

Official sources
→ Load and process documents
→ Chunk content
→ Create embeddings
→ Store embeddings in a vector database

The query pipeline should follow:

User question
→ Determine whether the question is in scope
→ Create query embedding
→ Retrieve relevant chunks
→ Provide retrieved context to the LLM
→ Generate a grounded answer
→ Attach the appropriate source citation

The same embedding model should be used for document chunks and user questions.

13. Transparency

The assistant should make the source of its factual answers visible to the user.

The source citation should come from the retrieved source metadata rather than being invented by the LLM.

The "Last updated from sources" date should also come from the source metadata.

14. UI Requirements

The interface should be intentionally small and simple.

It should include:
- A welcome line
- A short description of the assistant
- A clear note:
  "Facts-only. No investment advice."
- 3 example questions
- A question input
- An answer area
- One source link
- Last updated date

15. Out of Scope

The assistant will not provide:

- Investment recommendations
- Buy or sell decisions
- Portfolio construction
- Personalized financial advice
- Performance or return calculations
- Scheme ranking
- Personalized account information
- Transaction execution
- Portfolio tracking
- User account management
- Collection of personal information

16. Success Criteria

The prototype will be considered successful if:

- It answers factual mutual fund questions using the selected official source corpus.
- Retrieved information is relevant to the question.
- Answers are concise and limited to 3 sentences or fewer.
- Every factual answer contains one correct official source link.
- Every factual answer contains the "Last updated from sources:" line.
- Advice and portfolio questions are refused.
- PII and account-specific requests are safely blocked.
- Unsupported questions result in a clear "not found / cannot verify" response instead of a fabricated answer.
- No third-party source is used as factual knowledge.
- The RAG pipeline can be inspected and tested independently from the UI.

17. Known Limitations

This is a small-corpus prototype and is not intended to answer questions about every mutual fund in India.

Its answers are limited to information available in the selected 15–25 official sources and the four selected HDFC Mutual Fund schemes.

The assistant may not answer questions when the required fact is not present or cannot be reliably retrieved from the available source corpus.

18. Disclaimer

Facts-only. No investment advice.

This assistant provides factual information from selected official public sources and does not provide investment, financial, or portfolio advice.
